# 💫 About Me:
I’m a 23-year-old Data Science student with a strong business mindset and a clear ambition: to build a career at the intersection of analytics and investing. I’m currently completing a Master’s in Business Analytics and Data Science, where I combine technical curiosity with a growing passion for private equity. I’m drawn to the craft of understanding businesses deeply, supporting value creation, and learning how capital allocation decisions shape long-term outcomes.

My academic path reflects both resilience and self-awareness. I initially started in general engineering, but I realized the field wasn’t aligned with the future I wanted to build. Rather than forcing a direction that didn’t fit, I pivoted into business engineering in Brussels. I transferred prior coursework and accelerated my degree, graduating in two years instead of three. During that transition, I discovered a genuine interest in finance — which evolved into a broader commitment to financial literacy, investing, and understanding how markets connect to real companies.

That interest crystallized during a private equity back-office internship, where I had close exposure to the deal environment and the types of opportunities that move through a PE firm. The experience confirmed two things for me: private equity is the long-term direction I want to pursue, and I thrive in roles that are closer to analysis, decisions, and the businesses themselves. I’m especially motivated by the hands-on proximity to companies. Compared to purely market-facing trading approaches, I find it more meaningful to connect investment judgment to real operations, people, and strategy.

Because my current master’s program is highly technical and not directly finance-focused, I’ve taken ownership of filling that gap myself. I’ve pursued structured self-learning through online platforms — completing more than ten DataCamp courses alongside additional finance coursework — while maintaining strong academic performance in my program. I enjoy collaborative work and consistently contribute actively to group projects, earning positive feedback from teammates. At one point, I requested an official ranking certificate and learned that I was placed 3rd overall in my intake — an achievement that reflects both discipline and momentum.

Looking ahead, my plan is internationally oriented. I aim to begin my private equity career in a major European hub — particularly Luxembourg or Switzerland — and later expand into the Asian market. I have a strong interest in Japan and the broader region’s long-term growth potential. I’ve also explored exchange opportunities in places like Singapore and New York, motivated by a desire to understand how private equity is practiced across different financial ecosystems and to build a global network early in my career.

At my core, I’m driven by progress: learning quickly, staying open to feedback, and building a life where my work and curiosity reinforce each other. I approach advice with humility and discernment. One line that resonates with me comes from “Everybody’s Free (to Wear Sunscreen)” — to be careful whose advice you buy, but patient with those who supply it. That balance — ambitious but grounded, open yet intentional — captures how I’m building my next chapter.
